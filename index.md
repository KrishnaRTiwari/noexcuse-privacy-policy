# NoExcuse Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 17, 2026**

NoExcuse ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information NoExcuse collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use NoExcuse, we may collect and process the following information:

### Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may process information associated with your account, such as:

- Email address
- User account identifier
- Name or profile information that you choose to provide
- Authentication information handled through our authentication provider

### User-Generated Information

Information that you voluntarily provide while using the app may be stored so that the app can provide its features. This may include:

- Profile information
- Challenges, goals, or other content you create
- Alerts, preferences, or settings
- Other information you voluntarily submit within the app

### Profile Images and Uploaded Content

If you choose to upload a profile image or other supported content, the relevant file may be stored using Firebase Storage.

### Device and Notification Information

The app may process information necessary to provide push notifications, including a Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token associated with your device.

We do not use notification information to send advertising messages.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

- Creating and managing your account
- Providing the core functionality of NoExcuse
- Saving and displaying your profile and user-generated content
- Providing challenges, alerts, and notifications
- Maintaining and improving the app
- Preventing misuse and protecting the security of the service
- Responding to support requests
- Complying with applicable legal requirements

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Firebase Services

NoExcuse uses services provided by Google Firebase, which may process information on our behalf to provide application functionality.

These services include:

- Firebase Authentication for account authentication
- Cloud Firestore for storing application data
- Firebase Storage for storing supported uploaded files
- Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications

Firebase may process information according to its own privacy practices and applicable terms.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate NoExcuse, including Firebase services.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with applicable law or legal process
- Protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the service
- Detect, prevent, or investigate fraud, abuse, or security issues

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain user accounts and application functionality, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with our practices and applicable requirements.

## 6. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your NoExcuse account and associated personal data.

To request deletion, contact us using the support contact provided on the Google Play Store listing.

We will process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable requirements and our data-retention obligations.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

NoExcuse is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

## 9. Third-Party Services

NoExcuse relies on third-party infrastructure and services, including Google Firebase, to provide certain application functionality.

Those providers may process information in accordance with their own policies.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this policy.

We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information.

## 11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy practices, or requests regarding your personal information, please contact us through the support contact provided on the NoExcuse Google Play Store listing.
